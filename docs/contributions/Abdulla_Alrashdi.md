# Abdulla Alrashdi — HVAC optimization and control

## Contribution summary

Abdulla was assigned responsibility for HVAC optimization research and control strategies. This area covers alternative operating settings, comparisons between baseline and controlled operation, and the relationship between electricity consumption and occupant comfort. The project also investigated the suitability of real-building datasets for simulation and control research.

The earlier synthetic optimization studies provided a foundation for the team's later BOPTEST experiments. The control research helped establish the limitations that led to supervised operation in the final dashboard.

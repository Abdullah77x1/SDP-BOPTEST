# Draft inputs for official safety forms

**Project.** LLM-Enabled Intelligent Digital Twin for Smart Building Energy Management, COSC14, COSC 497. Software and simulation on standard university-approved computers. One simulated office and a benchmark software API. The final system has no physical actuator or building access.

Use the official Risk Assessment form on handbook pp. 13–14 and the Health and Safety Assessment form on p. 15. The Declaration on p. 16 is marked page 2 of 2 and is supplied separately for team signatures. These draft inputs support the assessment. University officials and the team must confirm the actual work location, activity dates, responsible individuals, likelihood, severity, residual risk, approvals and signatures.

| Activity or hazard | People and effect | Existing or proposed controls for assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Workstation use and extended screen time | Students and reviewers. Fatigue, eyestrain, posture discomfort. | Set suitable chair and monitor height, take breaks, use standard computer-work practices. Confirm the actual arrangements. |
| Normal computer power and cables | Team and nearby users. Trip or equipment hazard. | Approved equipment, no exposed conductors, tidy leads, stop using damaged equipment. |
| Incorrect software command, stale reading or unavailable history | Operator and demonstration. Unexpected simulated conditions or results. | Guard the supported action, verify readback and native release, record unavailable forecasts and use labeled fallback when possible, and retain an operator stop. |
| Access to records | Contributors and reviewers. Privacy or unauthorized access. | Private repository, approved access, no credentials or personal data in shared logs, separate storage for sensitive files. |
| Connection to a real building in future work | Occupants, staff and equipment. Temperature, equipment and security risk. | Reassess as a new activity. Require permissions, independent limits, qualified commissioning and human control before connection. |

For the Health and Safety Assessment, enter the actual location, work activities and relevant hazard categories. Record assessor and supervisor information in the official fields. Route the signed original as directed to the University Safety Officer and include the required report copy. Use the official risk matrix and leave the signatures and decision fields for the authorized participants.

The reproduced scope supports the software-only checkbox, subject to the supervisor confirming the actual activity. Verify the official electrical, radiation, laser, robotic and mechanical headings against the work performed. If the team confirms no appreciable risk for a heading, follow the form’s instruction to enter NONE. Record ordinary workstation and software risks in the appropriate area, and use an additional detail page if needed.
